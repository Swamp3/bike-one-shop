# legal-security-reviewer, 2026-11-16

Verdict: **needs changes** for the branch, **blocking for go-live** until the release-blocker list below is resolved. Not legal advice; a German e-commerce lawyer has to sign off. The reviewer compared the Widerrufsbelehrung with the model text from memory only. Tests, build and screenshots were not part of the review. Status of every finding: open, nothing fixed yet.

## Findings (code and content, fixable by a developer)

1. **Critical: no tax rate.** Tax region `de` has no rate, so VAT shows 0,00 € in cart, checkout, order page and the confirmation e-mail. The seed creates the region but no rate, and the tax-inclusive price preferences come only from `import-woo-catalog.ts`. Add an idempotent migration script: default 19 % rate on the region and tax-inclusive preferences for EUR and the region. Then check cart total, `tax_total` (about total/1.19*0.19), taxed shipping, and what the TriCon order upload sends (net or gross).
2. **High: AGB § 2 acceptance model.** AGB say the e-mail is not an acceptance and the contract is formed by a "Auftrags- oder Versandbestätigung", which does not exist; no acceptance deadline (§ 308 Nr. 1 BGB); `build-email.ts:136` reads like an acceptance; § 3 makes the price due before acceptance. Pick one model and align AGB § 2, § 3 and the e-mail.
3. **High: return costs for bikes** (estimate needed, § 357 Abs. 5 BGB). Owner decision, see `rueckfragen-rueckgabe-widerruf.md`.
4. **High: Widerrufsbelehrung deviates from the model.** The added section "Wann es kein Widerrufsrecht gibt" and the made-to-order paragraph can cost the model protection; phone number is missing (Gestaltungshinweis, "soweit verfügbar"). Keep the Belehrung verbatim, move remarks outside the block, drop the made-to-order paragraph unless configured builds are really offered, add a phone number. Applies to the page and `build-email.ts`.
5. **High: newsletter placeholder** says "Bestätigungsmail wurde verschickt" and promises unsubscribing, but nothing is sent. Hide it (`NEXT_PUBLIC_NEWSLETTER_ENABLED=false`) until the provider and double opt-in exist.
6. **High: price and shipping info.** "Verfügbar — ab Lager" and "Lieferzeit 3–5 Werktage" are static for every product. "Versandkosten werden im Warenkorb berechnet" vs. the cart's "im nächsten Schritt"; shipping is flat, so the amount should be stated. DB shows 100 (Standard) and 200 (Express) while TODO says 10 EUR: check which is meant. Label the cart total "Gesamt inkl. MwSt." and the VAT row "darin enthaltene MwSt.".
7. **Medium: order list.** The return window is counted from the order date; the statutory period starts at receipt (§ 356 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 BGB). The "Rücksendung im Store starten" button only sets local state. Remove the "abgelaufen" logic and the fake button, point to `/widerrufsrecht` and the contact e-mail.
8. **Medium: Impressum and Datenschutz reachability.** Links go to bike-one.org; the checkout layout has no footer or Impressum link. Add both to the checkout.
9. **Medium: confirmation e-mail can fail silently** in production without a provider (boot warning only) and in any non-production environment (`notification-local` sends nothing). Fail startup in production without a provider, or add an alert/retry; document that staging must not take real orders.
10. **Medium: e-mail content gaps.** No seller details (phone, HRB, USt-IdNr, Geschäftsführer); only an AGB link (§ 312i Abs. 1 Nr. 4 BGB: attach or inline the text); no billing address; links fall back to `http://localhost:8000` when `STOREFRONT_URL` is unset; the SendGrid provider drops the text part. Keep the Widerruf text in one source instead of three copies.
11. **Low:** AGB § 9 should say only "nicht bereit" (the Impressum does); "nicht verpflichtet" needs 10 or fewer employees (§ 36 Abs. 3 VSBG). The notification table stores recipient e-mails: add a retention entry to the Datenschutzerklärung. Price note on cards is 10.5 px: use 11 to 12 px.

## Legal flags

- **Withdrawal button (EU 2023/2673, Art. 11a Directive 2011/83/EU)**: reviewer believes it applies from 19 June 2026 and is not implemented (German § 356a BGB, not verified). Possible release blocker; ask the lawyer.
- **DSGVO Art. 13/28**: external Datenschutzerklärung does not cover the shop; processing agreements needed for Strato and the e-mail provider; prefer an EU mail provider (SendGrid is US).
- **GPSR**: structure is right, no manufacturer data for any of the 7 brands.
- **Old Impressum**: matches our company data; still cites § 5 TMG (now DDG) and the discontinued EU OS platform; "geschäftsführender Gesellschafter" should read "Geschäftsführer"; add a phone number.
- Not in code: LUCID/VerpackG, WEEE/BattG, BFSG applicability, PAngV § 11 before any crossed-out price.
- The known SumUp webhook signature gap still blocks real payments.

## Release blockers (owner side)

bike-one.org shutdown, new Datenschutzerklärung, corrected Impressum, return costs, manufacturer data, e-mail provider and processing agreement, LUCID/WEEE/BattG, VAT rate, real shipping prices and delivery times, AGB § 2 acceptance model, newsletter placeholder hidden, withdrawal button question.

Verified: no "30 Tage" or "2 Stunden" claims left; button label "Zahlungspflichtig bestellen" on all three payment paths; cookie inventory matches the code (except `_medusa_cache_id`, to confirm).
